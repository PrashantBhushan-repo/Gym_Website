# FitZone Membership Plans

FitZone offers membership plans designed for different needs and fitness goals.

## Basic Plan
- Price: ₹2,900 per month
- Includes:
  - Gym access
  - Basic equipment
  - Locker room access
- Excludes classes and personal training

## Premium Plan
- Price: ₹5,900 per month
- Includes:
  - 24/7 gym access
  - All equipment
  - Unlimited classes
  - 1 personal training session per month
  - Pool and sauna access

## Elite Plan
- Price: ₹9,900 per month
- Includes:
  - All Premium benefits
  - 4 personal training sessions per month
  - Nutrition plan
  - Priority booking
  - Guest passes

## Benefits Across Plans
- Access to modern gym equipment
- Support from certified trainers
- Online workout tracking
- Community motivation and support

## Contact and Bookings
- Call the gym or use the contact form to get started.
- Members can request personal training, group classes, or nutrition guidance.
