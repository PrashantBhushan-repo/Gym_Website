# Services and Classes

FitZone offers personal training and group fitness classes for all levels.

## Personal Training
- One-on-one coaching with certified fitness professionals.
- Customized workout plans tailored to individual goals, fitness levels, and schedules.
- Includes:
  - Initial fitness assessment
  - Customized workout plans
  - Nutrition guidance
  - Progress tracking
  - Flexible scheduling
- Members can book sessions through the contact page.

## Group Classes
A variety of classes are available to support fitness, flexibility, endurance, and strength.

### HIIT Training
- High-intensity interval training
- Burns calories and builds endurance
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Suitable for all levels

### Yoga Flow
- Improves flexibility, balance, and mental clarity
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Suitable for beginner to advanced participants

### Strength Circuit
- Builds muscle and increases strength with guided weight training
- Duration: 50 minutes
- Best for intermediate fitness levels

### Spin Class
- High-energy cycling workout
- Improves cardiovascular fitness
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Suitable for all levels

### Boxing Fitness
- Combines cardio and strength with boxing techniques
- Duration: 55 minutes
- Suitable for all levels

### Pilates
- Core-focused workout for strength and posture
- Duration: 50 minutes
- Suitable for all levels

## Getting Started
- View class schedules and book your first session through the contact page.
