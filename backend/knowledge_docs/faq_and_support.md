# FAQ and Support

This document covers frequently asked questions and support guidance for FitZone members.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are your operating hours?
- 24/7 gym access is available.
- Staff are present from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.

### Do you offer trial memberships?
- Yes, a 7-day free trial is available for new members.

### Can I freeze my membership?
- Yes, memberships can be frozen for up to 3 months per year for medical reasons.

### What should I bring to my first workout?
- Bring a water bottle, towel, gym clothes, and a positive attitude.

### Do you have showers and lockers?
- Yes, FitZone provides locker rooms with showers and sauna access.

### Is parking available?
- Free parking is available for all members.

### Can I bring guests?
- Yes, guests are welcome.
- Guest fees apply, and members get discounted rates.

## Support and Contact
