# Programs and Policies

This document summarizes FitZone fitness programs, nutrition guidance, and gym policies.

## Fitness Programs

### Weight Loss Program
- Combines cardio, strength training, and nutrition.
- Weekly structure:
  - 4-5 cardio sessions (30-45 minutes each)
  - 3 strength training sessions (45-60 minutes each)
  - 2 rest or active recovery days
  - Daily nutrition tracking and weekly check-ins
- Key principles:
  - Caloric deficit of about 500 calories
  - High protein intake (1.6-2.2g per kg body weight)
  - Progressive overload every 2 weeks
  - Hydration: 3-4 liters of water per day
  - Sleep: 7-9 hours per night
- Expected results: 0.5-1 kg weight loss per week.

### Muscle Building Program
- Progressive overload program using compound movements.
- Key principles:
  - Increase weight/reps every 1-2 weeks
  - Focus on compound lifts like squats, deadlifts, bench press, and rows
  - Allow 48 hours recovery between muscle groups
  - Eat 2.2g protein per kg body weight
- Sample weekly split:
  - Day 1: Chest & Triceps
  - Day 2: Back & Biceps
  - Day 3: Rest or light cardio
  - Day 4: Legs
  - Day 5: Shoulders & Abs
  - Day 6: Full body or weak points
  - Day 7: Rest

### Keto Diet Guidance
- Macronutrient breakdown:
  - 70-80% fat
  - 15-20% protein
  - 5-10% carbohydrates (20-50g net carbs/day)
- Foods to eat:
  - Meats, fatty fish, eggs, butter, cheese, nuts, seeds, healthy oils, low-carb vegetables
- Foods to avoid:
  - Sugar, grains, fruit (except berries), beans, root vegetables, processed foods

## Gym Policies

### Membership Policies
- 30-day cancellation notice required.
- Memberships auto-renew monthly.
- No refunds on unused months.
- Guest fee: ₹20 per visit for non-members.
- Members receive discounted guest rates.

### Facility Rules
- Proper gym attire is required.
- Wipe down equipment after use.
- Re-rack weights.
- No dropping dumbbells.
- Respect others' personal space.
- No cell phone use on the gym floor.

### Refund Policy
- 7-day cooling-off period for new memberships.
- Medical refunds considered on a case-by-case basis.
- No refunds for personal reasons.
- Credits may be available for extended absences.
